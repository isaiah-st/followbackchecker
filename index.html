How to get your export
  1. Instagram: Settings > Accounts Centre > Your information and permissions > Download your information.
  2. Choose "Some of your information" and tick only "Followers and following".
  3. Pick "Download to device", format JSON (not HTML), date range "All time".
  4. Instagram emails you when it's ready in a few minutes. Download the zipped file and drop it above. No need to unzip.
